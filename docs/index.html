The modes

Pick your fight.

Both modes are cards in the Roundtable's expedition list. Pick one, press Commence and the mod finds your rivals. Every other card stays a normal expedition.

Up to 6 players · Last one standing

Battle Royale

Drop into Limveld with up to five rivals. Loot, level up and hunt them down while the Night's Tide closes in. The last Nightfarer standing wins.

  • Fall to a rival and you are out. No respawn. Enemies, bosses and falls still give you the game's usual second chance.
  • Their gear is yours. A defeated rival drops their weapons and talismans where they fell.
  • No free intel. Rivals never show on your map or compass, and Sites of Grace go dark mid-fight.
  • No draws. The final circle closes to a single point. Once you are out, watch the players still alive.
1v1 · Best of three

Arena

A straight duel in Heolstor's arena. No boss, no map, no farming. Bring the build you made at the Sparring Grounds and prove it.

  • Even ground. Everyone at level 15, every weapon at max upgrade, full flasks.
  • Your build. You fight with your Sparring Equipment loadout, relics included.
  • 3-minute rounds. If time runs out, the player with more health takes the round.
  • Ultimates decide it. Ultimate Arts are locked until round 3, which starts fully charged.

One click to queue

Press Commence and wait. The match forms and the expedition starts with no more clicks.

Killfeed and scores

Every down and kill on screen, and everyone's kills and deaths after the match.

Practice with bots

Turn Bots on to fight AI Nightfarers offline, in either mode.

Private rooms

Share a code with friends in the launcher and meet only each other.

How to start

In the fight in three steps.

You need ELDEN RING NIGHTREIGN on Steam and a Windows PC. The launcher handles the rest.

I — DOWNLOAD

Get the launcher

One small Windows app. It finds your game, installs the mod and keeps both itself and the mod up to date.

II — SEAMLESS CO-OP

Add Seamless Co-op

The mod runs on top of it. If it is missing, the launcher opens its Nexus page and installs the zip you download.

III — FIGHT

Pick a card, press Commence

Press Play in the launcher. At the Roundtable, choose the Battle Royale or Arena card, set Solo and press Commence.

Windows may warn you. The launcher is not code-signed yet, so your browser or SmartScreen may call it uncommon. Keep the file, then choose More info > Run anyway on the blue "Windows protected your PC" box.

Your normal game stays normal. The mod only runs through the launcher, without anti-cheat and away from the official servers. Steam's own Play button still starts the regular game, and mod matches use Seamless Co-op's save, separate from your own.
